## What Lilly is

Lilly discovers, develops, manufactures, and markets human pharmaceutical products in one operating segment. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 - Business, p. 5](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm)

Its main therapeutic areas include cardiometabolic health, oncology, immunology, and neuroscience. Mounjaro is Lilly's tirzepatide medicine for type 2 diabetes, while Zepbound is its tirzepatide medicine for obesity. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, pp. 43-45 and 49](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm)

## What changed

Lilly's revenue grew from $34.124B in 2023 to $45.043B in 2024, a 32% increase; management attributed the 2024 increase primarily to Mounjaro, Zepbound, and Verzenio. [2024 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, pp. 41 and 48](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947825000067/lly-20241231.htm) The 2023 annual report reported revenue of $34.124B, up 20% from 2022. [2023 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 40](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947824000065/lly-20231231.htm)

In 2025, revenue rose another 45% to $65.179B. Mounjaro generated $22.965B, up 99%, and Zepbound generated $13.542B, up 175%; management said increased volume in these products drove the growth, partly offset by lower realized prices. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, pp. 43 and 49](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm)

Gross margin was 83.0% in 2025, compared with 81.3% in 2024. Management attributed the increase primarily to favorable product mix and improved production costs, partly offset by lower realized prices. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 50](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm)

The growth continued into Q2 2026: Lilly reported revenue of $23.0B, up 48% year over year, driven primarily by Mounjaro and Zepbound volume. [Lilly Q2 2026 earnings release, August 5, 2026, p. 1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-raises-full)

**My judgment:** The three-year revenue pattern and the 2026 quarterly update show unusually strong operating momentum, largely concentrated in the tirzepatide franchise. This is my interpretation of the cited evidence.

## Pipeline and recent developments

Lilly's 2025 filing listed orforglipron, retatrutide, insulin efsitora alfa, and multiple oncology, immunology, and neuroscience programs as submitted for regulatory review or in Phase 3 development. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, pp. 43-45](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm)

In August 2026, Lilly reported positive data from three additional Phase 3 retatrutide obesity trials and stated it planned a U.S. FDA submission in the first quarter of 2027. [Lilly Q2 2026 earnings release, August 5, 2026, p. 1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-raises-full)

On August 31, 2026, Lilly reported that the Taltz-and-Zepbound combination showed maintained or improved results through Week 52 in two Phase 3b studies, with no new safety concerns identified in that release. [Lilly news release, August 31, 2026, p. 1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/new-phase-3b-data-lillys-taltz-ixekizumab-and-zepbound)

Also on August 31, 2026, Lilly announced a definitive agreement to acquire Merida Biosciences, whose lead program MER511 was in Phase 1 for Graves' disease and thyroid eye disease. [Lilly news release, August 31, 2026, p. 1](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-acquire-merida-biosciences-advance-treatments-serious)

## Financial position and risks

At December 31, 2025, Lilly reported $7.268B of cash and cash equivalents and $42.503B of total debt. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 8 - Consolidated Balance Sheets, p. 59; Note 11 - Borrowings, p. 76](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm)

The 2025 Form 10-K warns that pharmaceutical research and development is costly and uncertain, that products can lose intellectual-property protection or face competing products, and that pricing, supply-chain complexity, global conditions, regulation, and foreign-currency changes can affect results. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 1A - Risk Factors, pp. 25-38; Item 7 - MD&A, pp. 45-48](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm)

Lilly's listed U.S. compound-patent expiry dates include 2026 for Cyramza, 2027 for Trulicity, 2029 for Jardiance, 2030 for Taltz, and 2036 for Mounjaro/Zepbound. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 1 - Intellectual Property Portfolio, pp. 11-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm)

**My judgment:** Lilly's debt is material but is paired with substantial cash generation and high gross margin; the larger risk is whether GLP-1 volume growth can stay ahead of pricing, competition, and patent risks. This is my interpretation of the cited evidence.
