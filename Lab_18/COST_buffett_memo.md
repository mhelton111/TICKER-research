# Buffett-style business memo — COST

**Company:** COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP /NEW  
**Verdict:** **own the business**

This is a business-quality judgment, not a price or buy/sell recommendation. ROE ran from 28.3% in the first reported year to 25.8% in the last reported year. Owner earnings rose from $3,501.0 million to $9,382.0 million across the reported period. The three code checks are: ROE of 15% or more every year — **pass**; owner earnings positive every year — **pass**; and owner earnings grew from the first year to the last — **pass**.

The annual filing facts support a review of earning power, but they do not establish a durable moat, management quality, or a sensible purchase price. Those are judgment questions that require evidence beyond this five-year fact table. No market-price data were used.
