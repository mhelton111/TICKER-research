### Reverse bridge check

```text
$1,077.155B synthetic enterprise value
- $42.503B total debt
+  $7.268B cash and cash equivalents
= $1,041.920B synthetic equity value

$1,041.920B / 0.8993B diluted shares = $1,158.59 per share
```

The reverse bridge reproduces the August 31, 2026 closing price, subject to rounding. The share price is from [ChartExchange historical prices](https://chartexchange.com/symbol/nyse-lly/historical/). Debt, cash, and diluted shares are from the [2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/000005947826000013/lly-20251231.htm). This is a synthetic bridge: the market-close date is later than the December 31, 2025 balance-sheet date, so it is a mechanical check rather than a standalone valuation conclusion.

## Consequential assumptions

- Demand for Mounjaro and Zepbound remains strong enough to support Lilly's future growth.
- Lower realized prices and reimbursement changes do not reduce GLP-1 profitability enough to impair the investment case.
- Lilly can fund its debt, manufacturing investment, and research pipeline without materially weakening financial flexibility.

## Unknowns

- Whether the August 31, 2026 share price already reflects more GLP-1 growth than Lilly can deliver.
- The long-term effect of competition, including Novo Nordisk and new obesity-treatment entrants, on market share and pricing.
- The financial impact of patent challenges, product-liability litigation, and regulatory changes.
- The future contribution and probability of success of Lilly's clinical pipeline.

## Research plan

1. Review Lilly's most recent 10-Q and earnings release to update revenue, cash, debt, and operating trends after December 31, 2025.
2. Compare Lilly's growth, margins, leverage, and valuation with Novo Nordisk, Merck, and Pfizer.
3. Research the durability of the Mounjaro and Zepbound franchises, including volume, pricing, reimbursement, supply, competition, and patent protection.
4. Review material legal and regulatory matters, including patent disputes and product-safety litigation.
5. Build a valuation range and compare it with the August 31, 2026 market price before revisiting the Watch-Defer thesis.
