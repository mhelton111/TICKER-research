## What Lilly does

Lilly is a pharmaceutical company selling medicines in cardiometabolic health, oncology, immunology, and neuroscience. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1—Business; Item 7—MD&A, Clinical Development Pipeline]

- **Mounjaro** is tirzepatide marketed for type 2 diabetes. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, Results of Operations]
- **Zepbound** is tirzepatide marketed for obesity. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, Results of Operations]
- Other examples of Lilly medicines include Verzenio in oncology, Omvoh in immunology, and Kisunla in neuroscience. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, Clinical Development Pipeline]

## Risks and unknowns

- Lilly warns that pharmaceutical research and development is costly, takes substantial time, and has a high failure rate. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, Clinical Development Pipeline]
- Lilly warns that products can lose intellectual-property protection or be displaced by competing medicines and therapies. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, Clinical Development Pipeline; Item 1—Business, Intellectual Property]
- Lilly reports patent protections that expire before or during 2030 for certain products, including Cyramza (2026), Trulicity (2027), Jardiance (2029), and Taltz (2030); its listed U.S. compound-patent expiry for Mounjaro/Zepbound is 2036. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K, Item 1—Business, Intellectual Property Portfolio]
- Lilly warns that pricing, supply-chain and labor complexity, inflation, global conditions, regulation, and foreign-currency movements can affect results. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K, Item 7—MD&A, Other Factors and Foreign Currency Exchange Rates]
