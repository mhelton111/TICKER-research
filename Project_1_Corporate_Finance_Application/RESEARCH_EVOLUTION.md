# Research evolution - Edition A to final analysis

## Unchanged baseline

`Edition_A_UNCHANGED.md` is the preserved Edition A baseline. It framed the decision as Watch-Defer and used FY2025 filing facts plus a synthetic enterprise-to-equity bridge. It did not make a final valuation conclusion.

## What changed through the project

| Evidence surface | What it added | Effect on decision |
|---|---|---|
| Lab 05/06 DCF | A simple FCFF DCF, sensitivity, and reverse-DCF practice | Identified that a growth-only approach was far below the preserved market price and that assumptions needed product-level support. |
| Lab 08 comparables | Pre-defined, qualified AMGN/GILD P/E policy; FY25 GAAP EPS basis | Produced a $493-$631 reference range, but highlighted peer-growth mismatch; it is not averaged with DCF. |
| Final model | Product-driver forecast, five-year three-statement articulation, causal cases, bridge, sensitivity, and market-implied diagnostic | Supports Watch-Defer instead of initiating: the low/base/high DCF range remains below the preserved market reference and depends heavily on terminal value. |

## What remained stable

The intended user is a buy-side committee with no LLY position. The key question remains whether sustainable cardiometabolic cash generation compensates for pricing, competition, investment, and valuation risk. FY2025 Form 10-K facts remain the historical base, and the model explicitly preserves the timing mismatch between that base and later price references.

## Why the final judgment is conditional

The final model improves the method, but does not eliminate uncertainty. Volume, price/rebate pressure, margins, capex, and terminal assumptions are all exposed and challenged. The decision changes only with new filed evidence that changes durable FCFF, or with a price that enters the defended range without deterioration in those drivers.
